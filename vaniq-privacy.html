VaniQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

VaniQ is a local-business platform developed by Vandorix Solutions. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you use VaniQ.

1. Information We May Handle

Depending on how you use VaniQ, the platform may handle information such as a person's name, mobile number, business name, shop or service details, address or locality, product or service information, photographs, order details, and information supplied during customer-seller communication.

VaniQ requests only information reasonably needed for the relevant feature or transaction.

2. Location and Local Business Information

VaniQ may use locality, district, state or other location information supplied by users or sellers to organize and display local businesses and services. Where device location permission is used by a VaniQ feature, the permission will be requested through the operating system and can be controlled by the user.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To create and display local business or service listings.
  • To help customers discover relevant local sellers and service providers.
  • To facilitate enquiries, orders and customer-seller communication.
  • To process or record order-related information, including Cash on Delivery (COD) requests where offered.
  • To maintain, secure and improve VaniQ.
  • To comply with applicable legal requirements.

4. Seller Listings

Information intentionally submitted for a seller listing may be displayed to other VaniQ users. Sellers should not publish passwords, financial account credentials, government identification numbers, or other confidential information in public listing fields.

5. Orders and COD

Where COD is available, VaniQ may handle the information needed to communicate the order between the customer and seller. VaniQ does not take ownership of products sold by independent sellers and does not itself become a party to the underlying sale unless expressly stated otherwise.

6. Data Storage and Architecture

VaniQ is designed around a practical, lightweight local-business model and does not require every seller to maintain a separate website. Certain operational information may be organized using tools such as Google Sheets or other service providers selected by Vandorix Solutions. The exact storage method may vary as the platform evolves.

7. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with the relevant customer, seller or service provider when necessary to perform a requested transaction or communication. Information may also be disclosed to service providers, authorities or other parties where required by law or necessary to protect the security and rights of users or Vandorix Solutions.

Vandorix Solutions does not sell personal information as a standalone product.

8. Third-Party Services

VaniQ may depend on third-party services such as hosting, mapping, analytics, messaging, storage or other infrastructure. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate business records, dispute handling, security, legal obligations or other lawful purposes. Users may request deletion or correction of information where applicable, subject to legal or operational requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

VaniQ is intended for general users and businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

11. Security

Vandorix Solutions takes reasonable measures to protect information. However, no electronic system or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when VaniQ's features, technology or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, correction or deletion requests, or other concerns relating to VaniQ, please contact Vandorix Solutions through the contact information published on the official website.
