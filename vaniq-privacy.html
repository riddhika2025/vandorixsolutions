Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 30 September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Vandorix Solutions handles information in connection with its products and services. We respect your privacy and aim to collect and use only the information necessary to provide the intended functionality of our products.

This policy currently applies to the following Vandorix Solutions products:

  • BIGULE – Offline Family Safety
  • VaniQ – Everything Local

Because these products provide different services, the types of information they handle and the way that information is used may differ. The relevant sections below explain those differences.

2. BIGULE – Offline Family Safety

BIGULE is a family-safety application designed to support communication between designated family members using SMS and device safety features. BIGULE is designed to provide core safety communication without requiring a cloud account or a Vandorix-operated cloud server.

Information and Permissions Used by BIGULE

📍 Location

BIGULE may access precise and approximate device location to provide location-related safety information, including GPS coordinates and location information used in safety messages.

💬 SMS

BIGULE uses SMS to send and receive safety-related messages, including heartbeat, SOS and other application-related communications.

📞 Phone & SIM Information

BIGULE may access phone state, SIM-related information and the phone number associated with a SIM when required for identification and family-safety functions.

👥 Contacts

BIGULE may access contacts when the user chooses to select or configure people for relevant family-safety communication features.

📲 Call Information

BIGULE may use phone-call state information to support safety features such as missed-call alerts. BIGULE does not require access to the call log for this function.

🔔 Notifications

BIGULE may display notifications for safety events, heartbeat status, SOS events, alerts and other application functions.

Location and Internet Use

BIGULE is designed to remain useful when an Internet connection is not available. Safety communication between BIGULE devices can use SMS.

When Internet connectivity is available, BIGULE may use network services for functions such as converting location coordinates into a human-readable address. This network-based address lookup is used to provide location information in a more understandable form.

Vandorix Solutions does not operate a cloud server that automatically stores BIGULE users' personal safety information.

BIGULE Local Data Storage

Necessary BIGULE application data and settings may be stored locally on the user's device.

BIGULE does not require users to create a Vandorix account or sign in with Google for its core safety functions.

BIGULE Safety Communication

BIGULE may transmit safety-related information through SMS to phone numbers selected or configured by the user for the relevant family-safety functions.

Depending on the feature being used, safety information may include location information, time, battery status, phone/SIM information, network information and other information necessary to describe a safety event.

The user is responsible for choosing the recipients of safety communications.

Background and Device Functions

BIGULE may use background device functions to support features such as periodic heartbeat messages, SOS alerts, device restart monitoring, SIM-change alerts and other safety functions.

BIGULE may also use notifications, alarms and foreground services to support these functions when permitted by the Android operating system.

Advertising and Tracking

  • BIGULE does not contain advertising.
  • BIGULE does not sell personal information.
  • BIGULE does not require Google Sign-In for its core functions.
  • BIGULE does not intentionally include advertising or tracking SDKs.

3. VaniQ – Everything Local

VaniQ is a local commerce and discovery application intended to help users discover and communicate with local shops, services and other community listings.

Information VaniQ May Handle

Depending on the features used, VaniQ may handle information such as:

  • Phone number used for identification or registration.
  • Name or other information voluntarily provided by the user.
  • Shop, seller or service information voluntarily submitted by users.
  • Product, catalogue or service information submitted by sellers.
  • Location or locality information when required for local discovery features.
  • Information voluntarily provided for optional community features.

How VaniQ Uses Information

VaniQ uses information to provide local discovery, seller, catalogue, communication and related application features requested by the user.

Information submitted by a seller or business may be displayed to other VaniQ users when necessary to provide the intended local discovery or commerce functionality.

VaniQ Data and Third-Party Services

Some VaniQ functions may use third-party infrastructure or services to process or transmit information required for the requested functionality. Where such services are used, information is processed for the relevant application purpose and according to the applicable service provider's terms and privacy practices.

4. Information We Do Not Sell

Vandorix Solutions does not sell users' personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant product functionality, maintain the service, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or fulfil another legitimate operational requirement.

Where information is stored locally on a user's device, the user may remove it by deleting the relevant application or by using applicable in-app functions.

6. Security

Vandorix Solutions takes reasonable measures to protect information handled by its products. However, no electronic transmission, device storage or digital service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. User Choices and Permissions

Users can control application permissions through their device settings. Some features may not function correctly if the permissions required for those features are denied or disabled.

8. Children's Privacy

Vandorix Solutions products may be used in family or community contexts. Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising the use of these products by children and for deciding which permissions and communication features are appropriate.

We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

9. Third-Party Services

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Users should review those policies where applicable.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The latest version will be published on this page together with the updated "Last Updated" date.
