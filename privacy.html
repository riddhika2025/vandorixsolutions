Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 30 September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Vandorix Solutions handles information in connection with its products and services. We respect your privacy and aim to collect and use only the information necessary to provide the intended functionality of our products.

This policy currently applies to the following Vandorix Solutions products:

  • BIGULE – Offline Family Safety
  • VaniQ – Everything Local

Because these products provide different services, the types of information they handle and the way that information is used may differ. The relevant sections below explain those differences.

2. BIGULE – Offline Family Safety

BIGULE is designed as a family-safety application that uses device capabilities and SMS communication to support safety-related functions.

Permissions and Information Used by BIGULE

📍 Location

Used to provide location-related safety information when a user enables and uses the relevant BIGULE safety features.

💬 SMS

Used to send and receive application-related and emergency safety messages initiated or received through BIGULE.

📞 Phone Functions

Certain phone and call-related information may be used to support BIGULE's family-safety and alert features.

BIGULE Data Storage

BIGULE stores necessary application information locally on the device. BIGULE is designed to operate without requiring a Vandorix-operated cloud server for its core safety communication functions.

Vandorix Solutions does not automatically upload BIGULE users' personal information to a Vandorix cloud server for storage.

BIGULE Sharing

BIGULE may transmit safety-related information through SMS to the phone numbers selected or configured by the user for the relevant family-safety functions.

The user is responsible for choosing the recipients of such communications and for ensuring that the selected recipients are appropriate.

Information BIGULE Does Not Use for Advertising

  • No advertising within BIGULE.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • No Google Sign-In required for the core application.
  • No advertising tracking SDKs are intentionally included in BIGULE.

3. VaniQ – Everything Local

VaniQ is a local commerce and discovery application intended to help users discover and communicate with local shops, services and other community listings.

Information VaniQ May Handle

Depending on the features used, VaniQ may handle information such as:

  • Phone number used for identification or registration.
  • Name or other information voluntarily provided by the user.
  • Shop, seller or service information voluntarily submitted by users.
  • Product, catalogue or service information submitted by sellers.
  • Location or locality information when required for local discovery features.
  • Information voluntarily provided for optional community features.

How VaniQ Uses Information

VaniQ uses information to provide local discovery, seller, catalogue, communication and related application features requested by the user.

Information submitted by a seller or business may be displayed to other VaniQ users when necessary to provide the intended local discovery or commerce functionality.

VaniQ Data and Third-Party Services

Some VaniQ functions may use third-party infrastructure or services to process or transmit information required for the requested functionality. Where such services are used, information is processed only for the relevant application purpose and according to the applicable service provider's terms and privacy practices.

4. Information We Do Not Sell

Vandorix Solutions does not sell users' personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant product functionality, maintain the service, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or fulfil another legitimate operational requirement.

Where information is stored locally on a user's device, the user may remove it by deleting the relevant application or using the applicable in-app functions, subject to the operating system and application behaviour.

6. Security

Vandorix Solutions takes reasonable measures to protect information handled by its products. However, no electronic transmission, device storage or digital service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. User Choices and Permissions

Users can control application permissions through their device settings. Some features may not function correctly if the permissions required for those features are denied or disabled.

8. Children's Privacy

Vandorix Solutions products may have features that can be used in family or community contexts. Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising the use of these products by children and for deciding which permissions and communication features are appropriate.

We do not knowingly use our products to request unnecessary personal information from children.

9. Third-Party Services

Our products may interact with device operating-system services, mobile-network services, messaging services, payment services, hosting services, or other third-party infrastructure where required for particular functionality.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Users should review those policies where applicable.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our products, services, technology, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be published on this page together with the updated "Last Updated" date.
